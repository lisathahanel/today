What Is an AI Image Generator?

An AI image generator is a tool that creates or modifies images using artificial intelligence. Most tools allow you to describe the image you want using a written prompt.

For example, you could enter the following prompt:

Create a professional photograph of a small business owner working on a laptop in a bright modern office, natural window light, realistic photography, clean composition, wide landscape format.

The system interprets your description and generates an image based on the model's capabilities and settings.

Some tools also support image editing, background replacement, image expansion, style changes, and the use of reference images. For bloggers, these features can reduce the time spent searching for suitable stock photographs or creating illustrations manually.

How to Choose the Right AI Image Generator

Instead of selecting a tool solely because it is popular, begin with the image you need to create.

For blog featured images

Choose a tool that can produce a clear, wide composition with enough space for your website's layout. Realistic scenes, clean illustrations, and simple conceptual visuals can all work well.

For YouTube thumbnails

Look for tools that create expressive subjects, clear focal points, and strong contrast. Add the final headline in an editor if the generator struggles with spelling or precise typography.

For social media designs

A design platform can be more convenient than a standalone generator because you may need multiple sizes, text overlays, logos, and consistent branding.

For educational illustrations

Prioritize clarity and accuracy over visual complexity. A diagram or simple illustration should explain the subject rather than distract from it.

For commercial projects

Read the provider's terms, including commercial-use rules, privacy settings, and restrictions on uploaded reference images. Do not assume that every free plan offers identical rights.

How to Write Better AI Image Prompts

The quality of an AI-generated image depends partly on how clearly you describe the intended result.

A vague prompt such as "make a picture about technology" leaves many important decisions to the model. A stronger prompt describes the subject, setting, style, composition, lighting, and intended use.

The Six-Part Image Prompt Formula

  1. Subject: What should appear in the image?
  2. Setting: Where is the scene taking place?
  3. Style: Should it look like a photograph, illustration, painting, or 3D render?
  4. Composition: What should be in the foreground, background, or center?
  5. Lighting and color: What mood or color palette should the image have?
  6. Output requirements: What aspect ratio, orientation, or text treatment do you need?

You do not need to include every element in every prompt. Use the details that matter for your particular image.

Example 1: Blog Featured Image

Create a professional editorial photograph for a blog article about artificial intelligence in digital marketing. Show a small business owner working at a laptop in a modern office, with subtle digital interface elements in the background. Natural lighting, realistic photography, warm neutral colors, uncluttered composition, landscape 16:9 format, no visible brand logos, no text.

This prompt defines the topic, subject, style, composition, and format.

Example 2: Finance Article Illustration

Create a clean editorial illustration for an educational article about budgeting. Show a notebook, calculator, coins, and a simple monthly budget chart on a tidy desk. Soft natural light, professional composition, restrained blue and green accents, landscape format, no readable text and no claims about financial outcomes.

For financial content, make sure the image does not imply that an investment or financial outcome is guaranteed.

Example 3: Technology Thumbnail

Create a high-quality technology thumbnail featuring a modern laptop displaying abstract AI visual patterns, professional dramatic lighting, a clear central subject, strong visual contrast, and empty space on the left for a headline to be added later. Wide 16:9 composition, no logos, no text.

Adding the headline afterward gives you more control over spelling and layout.

Step-by-Step: Create a Blog Featured Image With AI

Step 1: Identify the Article's Main Idea

Read the title and decide what visual would help readers understand the topic. For an article about AI writing tools, a laptop and writing workspace might be suitable. For a travel article, a relevant destination image may communicate the subject more effectively.

Step 2: Decide What the Image Must Communicate

Choose one main idea. Avoid trying to include every section of the article in a single image.

Step 3: Write a Detailed Prompt

Describe the subject, visual style, composition, lighting, and format. If you want the image to match your website's identity, specify a consistent color palette and visual approach.

Step 4: Generate Variations

If your free access permits multiple generations, create a few alternatives. Compare them for clarity, relevance, realism, and composition.

Do not select an image simply because it looks impressive. Choose one that accurately represents the article.

Step 5: Check for Mistakes

Inspect faces, hands, objects, signs, device screens, and any text. AI-generated images may contain distorted details or misleading visual elements. Regenerate or edit the image when necessary.

Step 6: Resize and Compress the Image

Use dimensions that suit your website. A 1200 × 675 pixel image is a reasonable starting point for many 16:9 featured images, but check your theme's requirements.

Export in an appropriate format, such as WebP or JPEG, where supported. Keep the file size reasonable without making the image visibly poor.

Step 7: Add Descriptive Alt Text

Alt text should describe the image's relevant content, not stuff it with keywords.

Example alt text: Small business owner using a laptop to create digital marketing content.

If an image is purely decorative, its accessibility treatment may differ depending on how it is implemented.

Step 8: Review Rights and Publish

Check the tool's terms for the image's intended use. Keep records of sources and significant editing where appropriate, particularly for commercial work.

Can You Use AI-Generated Images on a Monetized Blog?

AI-generated images may be suitable for a monetized blog, but the image itself does not guarantee compliance with advertising policies, copyright rules, or other legal requirements.

Focus on the complete publishing context:

  • Make sure the image supports the article rather than misleading readers.
  • Avoid copying recognizable copyrighted artwork or using protected characters without appropriate permission.
  • Do not fabricate product endorsements or imply that a person supports a product when they do not.
  • Check commercial-use terms for the selected tool and plan.
  • Use authentic screenshots or photographs when readers need to see a real product, test, or process.
  • Avoid using AI visuals to fabricate evidence, customer testimonials, or professional credentials.

For a website using advertising, page quality and the overall user experience matter too. Original, helpful content is more valuable than filling pages with decorative AI images.

Common AI Image Generation Mistakes

1. Writing Prompts That Are Too Vague

A broad prompt can produce a result that does not match your article.

Solution: Describe the subject, setting, style, composition, and intended use.

2. Trusting Every Generated Detail

An image may contain distorted objects, incorrect symbols, or implausible product features.

Solution: Inspect the final image before publishing.

3. Using the Same Image Style for Every Article

Repeated, generic visuals can make a website feel impersonal.

Solution: Maintain a consistent brand style while choosing imagery that reflects each article's actual subject.

4. Ignoring Image Size

Very large files can slow down pages, while excessively compressed images can look poor.

Solution: Resize and compress images appropriately for their display dimensions.

5. Adding Too Much Text Inside the Generated Image

AI-generated lettering may be misspelled, distorted, or difficult to read.

Solution: Generate the visual first and add final typography in a design editor when precise text matters.

6. Assuming Free Means Unrestricted

Free access, commercial rights, privacy, and ownership are different issues.

Solution: Read the relevant terms rather than assuming all generated images can be used in every context.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best completely free AI image generator?

There is no single best choice for everyone. Adobe Firefly, Ideogram, Leonardo AI, Canva, and Microsoft Designer are worth exploring, but free access and restrictions differ. Compare the current limits with the kinds of images you need to produce.

Can I use AI-generated images on Blogger?

Yes. You can add image files to Blogger posts using its normal image-upload workflow. Optimize file size, use descriptive alt text, and check the image's usage rights before publishing.

Are AI-generated images copyright-free?

Not necessarily. The legal status of AI-generated images varies by jurisdiction and depends on factors such as human contributions, source material, and the circumstances of creation. A provider's usage license is not the same as a guarantee that an image has no third-party rights issues.

Can I use AI images commercially?

That depends on the tool, plan, applicable terms, and image itself. Review the provider's current commercial-use rules and be particularly careful with recognizable people, protected brands, copyrighted characters, and uploaded reference images.

What size should a blog featured image be?

A 1200 × 675 pixel image is a useful starting point for a 16:9 layout. Your actual theme, page template, and social-sharing requirements may call for a different size or crop.

Do AI-generated images help SEO?

Relevant images can improve the reading experience and help illustrate a topic. Search visibility also depends on many other factors, including page usefulness, accessibility, image context, and technical implementation. Adding an AI-generated image does not automatically improve rankings.

Should I disclose that an image was generated by AI?

The answer depends on the context, applicable rules, and reader expectations. Disclosure may be appropriate when the image could be mistaken for documentary evidence, a real event, an authentic product photograph, or a genuine depiction of a person. Follow relevant platform and legal requirements.

The best AI image generator is the one that fits your creative task and publishing requirements.

Start with Adobe Firefly if you want image generation and editing, try Ideogram when text-heavy graphics are important, explore Leonardo AI for illustrations, and consider Canva when you need to turn an image into a finished design. Microsoft Designer is another option for quick AI-assisted visual ideas.

You do not need to use every platform. Choose one or two tools, test them with the same prompts, and compare the results. Pay attention to image quality, free limits, editing options, output dimensions, and usage rights.

For bloggers, the goal is not to generate as many images as possible. It is to create relevant, clear, original-looking visuals that make useful content easier to understand.

Practical tip for 2027: Recheck each provider's official features and terms before building your content workflow around a free plan. The most useful tool is one that continues to meet your needs for quality, reliability, and permitted usage.
